    Quill is in your menu bar

    Click into any text field, in Slack, Mail or your browser, and a small badge appears in its corner. It shows a checkmark when the text looks good, or how many things to fix. Click it, or press ⌥⌘G, to see the suggestions.

    Everything runs on this Mac. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

    The writing model

    Rewrites and the deeper check that catches mix-ups like “wit” for “with” use a language model that runs on this Mac. It’s a one-time download; spelling and grammar checks work without it.
