### New
- **Quill tells you when it has been updated.** The first time a new version starts, a notification says "Quill updated to 0.1.14", with a link to what's new.

### Updating
From 0.1.13: request Administrator Mode in the PaymentIQ Self Service app, then choose **Update to Quill 0.1.14…** (or **Check for Updates…**) in the menu bar menu and click **Install and restart**.

From an older version: quit Quill, open the dmg below, drag Quill to Applications and choose **Replace**. If macOS says *"Apple could not verify 'Quill' is free of malware…"*, click **Done** and run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Quill.app` in Terminal.
